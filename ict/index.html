ICT in Education

22 October to 15 November 2026 (end-semester exams from 16 November) · 0-0-4 (2 credits): 4 hours a week, about 2 in live sessions and about 2 on your own · fully online

This is the homepage for the course ICT in Education (HB302). This course prepares future school teachers to decide when technology helps learning, and to use it well: for preparing, teaching and assessing lessons, in online, blended and low-device classrooms, safely and inclusively.

  • The most important document is the Handbook. Read it completely: it has the teaching team, weekly plan, readings, capstone, assessment and course rules.
  • The FAQ keeps growing as the course runs. Look it up once in a while, and before you write to the teaching team.
  • New to the course tools? Start with Level 0.
  • Dates, sessions and what to hand in each week are in the Session plan.

Live classes: every Thursday and Friday, 11:00 to 11:50 AM (IST), online on Zoom.
