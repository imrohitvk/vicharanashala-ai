ICT in Education · get these ready in the first week

Before the course work begins, make sure you have everything below. Do them in this order. If you get stuck on any step, ask in the live session or email the teaching team.

1. A working laptop and phone

You need a working laptop (or desktop) and a phone, with a stable internet connection. The live sessions run on Zoom, and you will build your capstone on the laptop.

2. A Zoom account

Create a free account at zoom.us and install the Zoom app on your laptop or phone. Join with your full name, so that we can mark your attendance. Zoom links are sent in the teaching team's emails and in the course WhatsApp group.

3. Register on Spandan

We use Spandan for live polls and quizzes in the sessions, and those quizzes count for 15% of your marks. The Spandan link will be added here.

A short video on registering will be added here.

4. Sign up on ViBe

The lecture videos and their quizzes are on ViBe. Watch the ViBe student tutorial first, then sign up with the email you use for this course and look around the platform. The ViBe course registration link will be posted on the Announcements page by the end of the day on Monday, 26 October.

A short video on signing up will be added here.

5. Create a GitHub account

All your work is submitted on GitHub, and your capstone is published there. Create a free account at github.com. Videos on using GitHub are part of the ViBe course material.
