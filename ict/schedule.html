How each week works

  • Live sessions: Thursday and Friday, 11:00 to 11:50 AM (IST), online on Zoom.
  • ViBe lessons: short videos with quizzes. Watch them in your own time during the week. Your quiz answers count towards your grade (ViBe quizzes are 15% of the course), so answer each question carefully. First watch the ViBe student tutorial and sign up on ViBe. The ViBe course registration link will be posted on the Announcements page by the end of the day on Monday, 26 October.
  • Extra lab time: the course has 4 lab hours a week, and the two live sessions cover about 1 hour 40 minutes. The remaining time, about 2 hours 20 minutes a week, will be fixed together with the class.
  • Hand in: all work is submitted on GitHub only. Deadlines for each week's work will be announced here. The capstone is due on Sunday 15 November.

Zoom links are not posted here, because this page is public. Find them in the teaching team's emails or in the course WhatsApp group. Plans may change slightly; check Announcements.
