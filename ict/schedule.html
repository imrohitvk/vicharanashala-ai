How each week works

  • Live sessions: Thursday and Friday, 11:00 to 11:50 AM (IST), online on Zoom. Each session starts with 10 minutes for anything important: questions, announcements or a short discussion. The rest is work time on GitHub and your projects, with the teaching team there to help. A few short live polls come up during each session (15% of the course): they check that you are with us.
  • ViBe lessons: short videos with quizzes. Watch them in your own time. There is no weekly deadline: new lessons are added as the course goes on, and you can finish them any time before the course ends. Do each week's readings before that week's case studies; the ViBe lessons go over the same ideas again. Your quiz answers count towards your grade (ViBe quizzes are 15% of the course), so answer each question carefully. First watch the ViBe student tutorial and sign up on ViBe. The ViBe course registration link will be posted on the Announcements page by the end of the day on Monday, 26 October.
  • Your 4 hours a week: about 2 hours in the two live sessions, and about 2 hours on your own for the ViBe lessons, readings and case studies.
  • Hand in: your Tenali pull request and your capstone go on GitHub; case studies go on the case study page. The capstone is due on Sunday 15 November.

Zoom links are not posted here, because this page is public. Find them in the teaching team's emails or in the course WhatsApp group. Plans may change slightly; check Announcements.
