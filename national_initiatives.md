Vicharanashala operates at the intersection of pedagogical research and national educational policy. By leading the design and implementation of pivotal programs, the lab contributes to shaping a future where Indian higher education is both technologically advanced and deeply human-centric. Together, our national programmes have reached **3,500+ faculty** across India, with **600+ certified**.
